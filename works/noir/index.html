Questions, answered

How long does delivery take?

Watches in stock ship within two working days. Delivery inside Egypt takes two to four days, and orders to the Gulf and Europe take three to seven.

What does the warranty cover?

Five years on the movement and case against manufacturing defects. Straps and crystals are covered for the first year.

Can I return a watch?

Yes. You have 30 days to return an unworn watch with its tags and packaging for a full refund.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, bank transfer and instant payment. Cash on delivery is available in Cairo and Giza, and installment plans of up to 24 months run through partner banks.

Do you offer engraving?

We engrave up to 24 characters on the caseback at no charge. Add your text in the order notes at checkout.
