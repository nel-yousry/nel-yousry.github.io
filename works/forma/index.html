How a project unfolds

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Listen

    Workshops, audits and customer interviews. We learn what the brand is and what it should become.

  2. Weeks 3–4

    Define

    Positioning, voice and a clear idea to design against, agreed before any logo is drawn.

  3. Weeks 5–9

    Design

    Identity, system and applications, shown in context and refined through two focused rounds.

  4. Weeks 10–12

    Launch

    Guidelines, asset library and a handover session, plus 60 days of support after go-live.
