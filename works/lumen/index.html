    A studio built around patience

    Lumen began in a rented room with one north-facing window. Eleven years on, we are still a team of three: two photographers and a retoucher who care more about how a frame feels than how many frames we deliver.

    We don’t use flash for portraits and we don’t over-process. You get a gallery that looks like you on your best, most relaxed day.
