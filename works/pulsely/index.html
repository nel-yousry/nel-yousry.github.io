    Questions, answered

    Do I need a cookie banner?

    No. Pulsely does not use cookies or store personal data, so in most regions you can skip the banner.

    What counts as an event?

    Any page view or action you track. Plans are based on events per month, and we warn you before you reach your limit.

    Can I export my data?

    Yes. Export raw events as CSV at any time, or send them to your warehouse on the Scale plan.

    Can I cancel whenever I like?

    Yes. Cancel in two clicks from your billing page. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata within 30 days.
